## Brand & Style

This design system repositions the corporate image from a dense, heavy dark-mode aesthetic toward an airy, luminous, high-credibility architectural visual language. Tailored for enterprise-scale renewable energy infrastructure, workforce management, and photovoltaic assembly, the style balances industrial rigor with human dignity.

The design movement combines **Minimalism** with subtle **Technological Precision**. It prioritizes vast, breathable white fields, crisp structural lines, soft daylight shadows, and vibrant sun-charged accents. The resulting interface communicates institutional reliability, environmental optimism, and professional engineering excellence.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from expansive outdoor solar installations under clear sky conditions:

- **Primary (`#0284C7` - Sky Cyan/Blue):** Represents clean technology, open atmosphere, and engineering clarity. Used for primary interactive triggers, active navigation markers, and focus outlines.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B` - Solar Amber):** Evokes solar radiation, craftsmanship warmth, and energy creation. Used sparingly for key value propositions, accent tags, status indicators, and metric highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#0F172A` - Deep Industrial Navy):** Anchors headlines, primary text, dark action buttons, and footer sections requiring grounding contrast.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` - Slate Neutral):** Provides balanced legibility for descriptive body copy, secondary metadata, and icon strokes.
- **Surfaces & Borders:** 
  - Canvas: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) and Slate Tint (`#F8FAFC`).
  - Subtle Dividing Lines: Calibrated border slate (`#E2E8F0`).

## Typography

The typography uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all hierarchies to guarantee high legibility, modern technological poise, and geometric balance.

Headings leverage tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and confident font weights (`700` and `800`) to assert leadership in the industrial clean-energy sector. Body copy preserves a generous line-height multiplier (~1.6x) with relaxed letter spacing to maximize readability in technical service descriptions. Labels and category pills use uppercase or tightened semibold weights for instant visual parsing.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive fluid grid governs desktop viewports (max-width `1280px`), adapting to an 8-column system on tablet viewports and a 4-column system on mobile devices.

Section padding embraces deliberate luxury through generous vertical whitespace (`margin-lg` or 80px–112px between major landing bands), replacing the crowded visual rhythm of dark dashboards with expansive daylight clarity. Element grouping within cards adheres strictly to an 8pt base grid rhythm (`0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2.5rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through soft multi-tier tonal layers and diffuse ambient shadows rather than harsh borders or dark backdrops:

- **Base Ground:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with interspersed neutral bands (`#F8FAFC`) to delineate narrative modules without harsh dividers.
- **Card Surfaces:** Crisp white cards resting on `#F8FAFC` backgrounds, defined by delicate low-contrast outlines (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
- **Ambient Sun Shadow:** Floated elements and hover-state cards employ a warm-tinted soft elevation: `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Top Navigation Bar:** Frosted glassmorphic backdrop filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px); background-color: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85);`) with a subtle bottom hairline (`#E2E8F0`).

## Shapes

The design uses a balanced rounded geometry (`roundedness: 2` = `0.5rem` / `8px` base):

- **Buttons & Small Badges:** `8px` corner radius or fully rounded pill shapes for primary interaction chips.
- **Service & Feature Cards:** `16px` (`rounded-lg`) corner radius, projecting an approachable, modern enterprise feel.
- **Hero & Media Containers:** `24px` (`rounded-xl`) soft corners for imagery showcasing solar arrays and field teams.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Industrial Navy (`#0F172A`) with White text, transitioning to Sky Blue (`#0284C7`) on hover. Height 48px, horizontal padding 24px, medium font weight with smooth scale transitions.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background, 1.5px border in `#E2E8F0`, dark slate text (`#0F172A`), expanding to `#F8FAFC` background on hover.
- **Accent Action:** Solar Amber (`#F59E0B`) fill with deep navy text for high-conversion contact CTA targets.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- Pure White background (`#FFFFFF`), `16px` rounded corners, `1px solid #E2E8F0`. 
- Padding: 32px internal spacing.
- Interactive states: 4px upward translation (`transform: translateY(-4px)`) coupled with soft ambient shadow expansion and primary sky-blue accent on title text or icon background.

### Metric & Scale Badges
- Scale tier cards (e.g., "10 Personas", "20 Personas") feature large numerical values in `display-xl`, a secondary amber tag for availability, and an immediate high-contrast button.
- Value badges use soft tinted backgrounds (e.g., `#E0F2FE` sky tint or `#FEF3C7` amber tint) paired with saturated label typography.

### Input Fields & Newsletter Forms
- Light slate input fill (`#F8FAFC`), bordered with `#CBD5E1`, transitioning to Sky Blue (`#0284C7`) ring with 3px diffuse focus shadow.
- Neutral-600 placeholders with clear label placement above the field.

### Ethics & Values Slider / Grid
- Clean white architectural cards with monochrome or sky-tinted minimal SVG iconography, eliminating obsolete dark boxed tiles in favor of accessible, high-legibility enterprise cards.
